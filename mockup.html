Cost: A is one pseudo-element and one keyframe on markup that already exists, styled only under the sticker theme, so it is inert on dark. B restyles the card itself, which means the dark theme needs its own answer for what the card looks like — more work, and it touches a component that currently behaves identically in both themes.

Reduced motion is handled in both. Every animation on this page has a prefers-reduced-motion counterpart that shows the end state with no movement — the packet is already open, the stickers are already on the page, the stamp is already down. Turn it on in your OS and reload to check. All eleven of the app's existing keyframes have the same, and it is the easiest thing to lose in a playful pass.
